Engagement Models

Retained Advisory & Execution

Ongoing senior expertise for consistent leadership and delivery support.

Designed for companies that require continuous technical guidance alongside hands-on execution. This model provides stability, context continuity, and direct access to senior decision-making.

What’s included

  • Dedicated fractional capacity (30 hours per month)
  • Strategic guidance across architecture, product, and engineering
  • Hands-on execution support where needed
  • Priority access and ongoing continuity
  • Structured monthly reporting and forward planning

Pricing

  • $10,000 USD / month
  • Minimum 3-month engagement

Project-Based Engagement

Defined scope, clear outcomes, and accelerated delivery.

Designed for initiatives with specific objectives and timelines. This model ensures focused execution, accountability, and measurable results.

What’s included

  • Clearly defined scope, deliverables, and timeline
  • Milestone-based execution and reporting
  • Senior-led delivery with tailored support as required
  • Direct ownership of technical direction and outcomes
  • Knowledge transfer and documentation upon completion

Pricing

  • Starting at $20,000 USD per engagement
  • Final pricing based on scope, complexity, and timeline
